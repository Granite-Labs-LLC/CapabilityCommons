## Core

Electricity can cause shock, electrocution, burns, fires, and explosions. This is safety awareness information, not training to work on electrical equipment: only qualified electricians trained in safe lockout procedures should maintain electrical equipment.

## Topic additions

- Work on equipment: Before anyone works on electrical equipment it must be deenergized, with lockout and tag procedures used to keep it deenergized; ground-fault circuit interrupters (GFCIs) are used in wet locations.
- Shock emergency: If a person is "frozen" to a live electrical contact, shut off the current immediately. If you or someone else receives a shock, seek emergency medical help immediately.
- Burns: Electrical burns are among the most serious burns and require immediate medical attention; arc or flash burns should also be treated promptly.
- Overhead lines: People not qualified to work with electricity, and mechanical equipment, must stay at least 10 feet (3.05 meters) away from overhead power lines.

## Core safety_boundary

Botulism risk. Low-acid foods (vegetables, meats, poultry, seafood) must be processed in a pressure canner, never a boiling-water canner; tomatoes must be acidified to pH 4.6 or lower with lemon juice or citric acid as a tested recipe directs. Vent the canner 10 minutes before pressurizing, use the exact process time and pressure for your jar size, pack style, canner type, and altitude, and check dial gauges for accuracy every year. Use only tested recipes and never shorten process times. Do not taste food from an unsealed jar or one showing spoilage (bulging lid, spurting liquid, unnatural odor, mold): treat it as having produced botulinum toxin, which can be fatal whether swallowed or through skin contact, and follow USDA disposal and detoxification steps. If any deviation from USDA-tested methods may have occurred, boil low-acid and tomato foods in a saucepan before eating: 10 minutes below 1,000 ft, plus 1 minute for each additional 1,000 ft.
